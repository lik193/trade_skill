# Critique a trading thesis

Grant the implementation its stated behavior. Reconstruct the strongest case for taking the trade. Which risk or service gets paid, who might pay, and what would have to remain true through exit? A strategy earning an insurance premium can have a sound thesis, provided the price compensates for the loss it absorbs.

Choose the most credible rival explanation for these returns. When given totals, construct a different sequence of trades that produces them. Equal weekly P&L could hide offsetting daily bets. Identify the cheapest observation that separates the stories, such as timestamped position changes. For a tradable comparison, match the exposure, opportunity set, hold period and costs that matter.

Find a plausible path where the strategy loses despite getting its headline forecast right. Estimate loss per unit and across positions sharing that shock. Where relevant, compare what a gate protects against with expected payoff forgone on rejected trades. Keep uncertainty visible when rare losses or scant independent events dominate.

Give a decision in the user's stated research, simulation or deployment setting. A surviving thesis deserves a concrete next experiment. An ordinary risk premium deserves comparison with a cheaper way to hold it. A failed thesis deserves a stopping point. Say what evidence could reverse the decision and label any proposed comparison that still needs data.
